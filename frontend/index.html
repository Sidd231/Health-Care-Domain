Common Questions

Clinical & Technical FAQ

Q1

How accurate are the predictive models?

The Cardiovascular Risk Profiler utilizes an optimized Random Forest classifier with an evaluated cross-validation accuracy of 79.96%. The Diabetic Readmission model utilizes an XGBoost multi-class classifier evaluated on 29 standardized clinical features.

Q2

Does this replace a medical consultation?

No. This platform is a clinical decision-support architecture intended to assist healthcare practitioners and inform patients. Outputs complement certified clinical judgment and should always be discussed with a licensed physician.

Q3

How is patient biometric privacy protected?

All data processed through our REST API undergoes in-memory tensor transformation with no unencrypted persistence of protected health information (PHI), following HIPAA technical safeguard standards.
